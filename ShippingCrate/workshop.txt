Deliveries by shipping container, ordered on a radio. Hold your delivery radio and one of DayZ Expansion's airdrop planes heads for where you stand. What comes down under parachutes is a full-size shipping container, lit up with rotating beacons. Open the doors: a fitted-out, fuelled car, nose to the doors. Too big for a container (the M3S truck, the M1025, the helicopters)? It rides down on a pallet deck instead. Near the sea or a pond? Order a boat: it splashes down on the water beside you and its pallet sinks. No infected at the drop. Expansion's own airdrops are left as they are.

Every player always has a bright orange delivery radio: every new character gets one, and anyone joining without one is given another. Click it to pick the vehicle (every vanilla car, truck and motorbike, and with DayZ Expansion Vehicles the Land Rover, UAZ, Vodnik, bus, tractor and helicopters), hold it to order that vehicle dropped where you stand. One delivery at a time each.

Or order Equipment: a container with a hand truck, a barrel of guns (VSD, LAR, KA-M, SCR 16 and SVAL, loaded, with spare magazines), a barrel of medical supplies and food, two barrels of clothing and gear, a barrel of base-building tools and kits, a barrel of field gear (night-vision goggles, head torch, binoculars, lockpick, screwdriver, every repair kit), a belt with a canteen, a sheathed knife and a holster, a barrel of explosives (C4, IEDs and detonators, claymores, land mines, grenades and smoke).

Drops stay until the next server restart and are cleared away then. Server owners can turn on a self-destruct instead (selfDestructMinutes): a teddy bear on a stack of IEDs on the roof, whose eyes light up as it shouts "Evacuate!" in Russian before it all goes up, along with everything still in or on it.

What comes down is a JSON file the server writes on its first start (profiles/ShippingCrate/ShippingCrate.json): any vehicle with any parts, anything in its boot, anything on the floor.

Requires DayZ Expansion Missions (and its Core, CF, Dabs Framework). Load on server and clients with -mod= (players need it to see the drop). The hand truck comes from our Hand Truck mod; without it, that one item is skipped.
