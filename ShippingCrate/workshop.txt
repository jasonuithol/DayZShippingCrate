Expansion's airdrops, delivered by shipping container. When an Expansion Missions airdrop starts, the plane heads for the middle of the biggest group of players instead of a fixed town, and what comes down under two parachutes is a full-size shipping container. Open the doors: a fitted-out, fuelled Ada, nose to the doors, with four SCR 17s (ACOG, stock, light, loaded) and spare magazines in the boot, a bottle of petrol, a lighter and a rope, and a hand truck behind it. No infected at the drop.

What's in the container is a JSON file the server writes on its first start (profiles/ShippingCrate/ShippingCrate.json): any vehicle with any parts, anything in its boot, anything on the floor, several loads picked at random by weight.

Requires DayZ Expansion Missions (and its Core, CF, Dabs Framework). Load on server and clients with -mod= (players need it to see the falling container). The default load's hand truck comes from our Hand Truck mod; without it, that one item is skipped.
